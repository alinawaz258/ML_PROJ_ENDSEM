AI-Based Tomato Leaf Disease Detection

Ali Nawaz

SRM Institute of Science and Technology

🔍 1. Abstract

Tomato diseases severely impact agricultural yield. Early, automated detection via imagery is crucial for crop management.

This research evaluates a full pipeline comparing Classical Machine Learning (using handcrafted features) against modern Deep Learning architectures to classify healthy and diseased tomato leaves.

📊 2. Dataset Overview

The PlantVillage subset for Tomatoes was utilized, containing varied disease presentations.

  • Total Images: 16,011
  • Classes: 9 Diseases + 1 Healthy
  • Split: 70% Train, 15% Val, 15% Test
Figure 1: Sample images across the 10 classes

⚙️ 3. Feature Extraction (Classical ML)

To train models like SVM and Random Forest, we extracted geometric and textural features:

  • HOG: Edge gradients and shapes.
  • LBP: Local surface texture patterns.
  • Color Hist: Disease discoloration mapping.

PCA Reduction: The massive 1,886-dim feature vector was compressed to 50 principal components to optimize model training speed.

🧠 4. Methodology

A. Regression (Lab 1 Baseline)
Attempted continuous prediction of class indices. Proved that regression is incompatible with categorical diseases (Acc: 15%).

B. Classical Classifiers (Lab 2)
Evaluated Bayesian Logistic Regression vs. SVM (RBF) on the PCA-reduced feature space.

C. Deep Learning
Trained directly on $128 \times 128 \times 3$ raw pixels.

  • Custom CNN: Built from scratch.
  • EfficientNetB0: Transfer learning applied to a state-of-the-art architecture.

🔬 5. Lab 2: Bayesian LR vs SVM

SVM significantly outperformed Bayesian LR. The RBF kernel successfully mapped the complex, non-linear boundaries of the disease features, whereas Logistic Regression was limited to linear boundaries.

Figure 2: Accuracy Comparison (Lab 2)

🏆 6. Final Benchmarking Results

Deep learning networks learning raw pixel hierarchies completely outperformed handcrafted classical features.

Model Accuracy F1-Score
EfficientNetB0 96.42% 0.96
Custom CNN 86.14% 0.86
SVM (RBF) 81.18% 0.81
Gradient Boosting 70.77% 0.70
Bayesian LR 65.65% 0.65

🚀 7. Conclusion & Deployment

Key Finding: Automated feature extraction via deep CNNs (EfficientNetB0) achieved state-of-the-art accuracy (96.4%), proving highly robust against lighting and texture variations.

Deployment: The final model has been successfully deployed as an interactive Streamlit Web App, allowing users to upload leaves for instant, AI-driven diagnostics.
